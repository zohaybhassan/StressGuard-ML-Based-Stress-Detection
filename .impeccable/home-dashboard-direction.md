THESIS: StressGuard is a calm instrument panel for a person checking their body in a real moment, not a gamified fitness dashboard. It refuses dense grids, competitive scores, and decorative wellness imagery that competes with the current reading.

OWN-WORLD: A near-black teal and midnight-blue field holds layered translucent cards with quiet borders. Mint identifies stress and primary actions; rose, cyan, and violet identify heart rate, steps, and sleep. Rounded geometry, precise vector icons, generous negative space, and crisp system typography make the interface feel trustworthy and native.

STORY: The user is greeted personally, immediately sees the current stress estimate and its status, verifies the supporting vitals and data freshness, then chooses a useful next action such as reviewing trends, entering workout mode, or talking to the assistant. Missing and disconnected data remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Below the system bars, a compact personalized header leads directly into the dominant current-stress card. The gauge and status own the visual center; supporting connection and freshness information stays secondary. Vitals follow as compact cards, then the existing insight and action surfaces. The persistent three-item navigation remains anchored at the bottom.
